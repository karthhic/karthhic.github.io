How lines are read
  • A line starting with a weekday starts a new day: Sunday 8/3/2026. Add *note after it for a flag.
  • A line starting with a time becomes a timed stop: 0945 Hrs Take-off, 1050 Landing or 09:45 ….
  • A line starting with ❗ or ! is highlighted as a warning.
  • An indented line is a note under the line above it.
  • Anything else is a task for that day.
