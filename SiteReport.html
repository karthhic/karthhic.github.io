How lines are read
  • A line starting with a weekday starts a new day: Monday 9/3/2026.
  • The first time range under a day is the hours on site: 8:30 to 17:35 or 0830-1735. Hours are totalled.
  • A line starting with # is an issue. Put its status in brackets at the end: (Fixed), (Fixed, but missing nut). No bracket means it's still open.
  • Text after * on a line becomes a note under that task.
  • An indented line is a note under the line above it.
  • Anything else is a completed task.
