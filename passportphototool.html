Passport Photo Tool

Crops a portrait to the Finnish Police passport photo rules (2020): exactly 500 × 653 px JPEG, max 250 KB. Everything runs in your browser and the photo never leaves your device.

1 Load photo & mark crown and chin

Drop a photo here, paste it, or click to open
Use a photo taken by a camera at 1.5 m or more. A phone held close distorts the face (rules, picture 9).

The crown is the top of the skull, not the top of the hair. The chin is the bottom of the chin, not the beard. Estimate them if hair or a beard hides them. Place both markers on the centre line of the face.

2 Adjust & export

Geometry
Global corrections (whole image only)

The rules forbid any edit that changes even the smallest detail of your appearance, including digital make-up. This tool only crops, scales, rotates and applies uniform tone/colour changes to the whole image. It has no retouching or background replacement on purpose.

3 Compliance

Load a photo and place both markers.

    Automatic background and exposure checks are estimates from image samples. Hard limits (size, format, file size, head size and position) are measured exactly. Download stays disabled until all of them pass.
